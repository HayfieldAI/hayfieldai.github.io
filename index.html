October 2026

Hayfield is closed, and it is for sale.

Hayfield let tile and stone dealers put their own products on a photo of a customer's room, price the result, and send it for approval. The software works. It did not find enough paying shops to keep going, so we have stopped running it, and the whole of it is available to someone who can take it further.

Make an offer or write to hayfieldai@gmail.com

The product, in 70 seconds

What is for sale

01

The product

A working app for tile and stone dealers: renders matched to a real product swatch, a material library with CSV import, customer records, branded proposal links, team workspaces, Razorpay billing, coupons and a staff admin panel.

02

The codebase

Three repositories with their full history: a FastAPI and Postgres backend with migrations and tests, a React and TypeScript web app, and the Astro blog. About 700 commits, with the operating notes and CI already written.

03

The domain

hayfield.in, registered until May 2027 and transferable to a buyer. Built India-first, with UPI-ready billing, for a trade where dealers sell on WhatsApp.

How it ran

Every service has been switched off and the code is untouched, so a buyer deploys it on their own accounts. The notes say how, including the traps that already cost a day.
